1. Summary

ClipNote is designed to store user content primarily on the device. The app does not require account registration and does not sync user data to a developer-operated cloud service.

2. What data the app handles

Depending on how you use the app, ClipNote may handle the following categories of data:

  • Clipboard text that you copy on your device while the app is active or resumed.
  • Notes and task content that you create manually in the app.
  • Reminder metadata such as due dates and local notification settings.
  • Backup files that you choose to export or import.
  • Purchase state related to the one-time Pro upgrade.
  • Advertising-related signals required by the Google Mobile Ads SDK when a rewarded ad is shown.

3. How clipboard data is used

ClipNote uses clipboard text only to provide clipboard history and related productivity features such as search, favorites, pinning, saving to notes, and backup/export.

Clipboard content is intended to remain on your device unless you explicitly export, share, import, or back up data yourself.

4. Local storage and backups

Notes, tasks, clipboard history, and settings are stored locally on the device. If you export a backup or import a backup file, that action is initiated by you and the file is written to or read from a location you select.

5. Ads and in-app purchases

ClipNote may show rewarded ads for specific optional features and may offer a one-time Pro purchase. These capabilities use third-party Google services, including:

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK
  • Google Play Billing / in-app purchase services

These Google services may process device identifiers, diagnostics, transaction data, or ad-related signals according to Google’s own policies.

6. Data sharing

The developer does not sell your personal data. The app does not provide user-created clipboard, note, or task content to a developer-operated cloud backend.

Limited data may be processed by Google services when ads are displayed or purchases are processed.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your locally stored content remains on your device until you delete it, clear app data, uninstall the app, or overwrite it with imported content. Exported backup files remain wherever you choose to store them.

8. Children

ClipNote is not specifically directed to children. If local laws require parental consent for certain processing, the user is responsible for complying with those laws before using the app.

9. International users

ClipNote may be distributed globally through Google Play. Any ad delivery or billing-related processing by Google may occur in different countries based on Google’s infrastructure and policies.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect app changes, legal requirements, or Google Play policy updates. The newest version should always be published at the privacy policy URL used in the Google Play listing.
